    How close to real hearing is this?

    Less close than the picture view. Hearing research measures the quietest sound heard and how finely pitches and directions are told apart; it cannot record what sound is like for a baby. The settings follow coarse tables that match infant threshold data at 3 and 6 months and are milder than the data at 1 month and from 12 months (code).

    A simulation built from published averages for groups of babies, not a medical test. If you are worried about a baby's seeing, hearing or development, talk to a paediatrician or health visitor.

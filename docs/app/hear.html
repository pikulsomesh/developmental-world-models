Hear like a baby

Babies hear from birth, but quiet and high sounds are harder for them to catch and it is harder to tell where a sound comes from. Play a sound both ways and compare. Headphones help.

    How honest is this?

    Less than the vision view. Hearing research measures thresholds (the quietest sound heard) and how finely pitches and directions are told apart; it cannot record what sound is like for a baby. The settings here are coarse and follow the paper's hearing tables, which are documented placeholders (code). Recordings stay in this browser tab and are never uploaded.

    Not a medical test. This is a simulation and research tool built from published averages. Babies vary widely and develop at their own pace. If you are worried about a baby's seeing, hearing or development, talk to a paediatrician or health visitor.

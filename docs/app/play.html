Activities and words

Is a baby of this age likely ready for an activity, and which words do babies usually understand by now? Open any activity to see the abilities it needs.

Activities

"Likely ready" means every ability the activity needs is typical by this age; "Emerging" means they are on their way. These are rule-based estimates from typical ages, not a measurement of any baby. Trying an activity early does no harm: babies show you what they enjoy.

Words

Word ages are rough tiers in the spirit of large parent-report studies (Wordbank; Frank et al. 2021) and lab tests of early word knowledge (Bergelson & Swingley 2012). They are guides to when many babies understand a word, and they differ between languages and families.

Not a medical test. This is a simulation and research tool built from published averages. Babies vary widely and develop at their own pace. If you are worried about a baby's seeing, hearing or development, talk to a paediatrician or health visitor.

Infant senses

Drag the age slider. One moment of one scene is passed through each sense's developmental front end. These filters are fixed functions of age, not learned: they define what the models receive. Vision changes most; touch and proprioception are close to mature at birth.

Hearing: left and right ear spectra (32 frequency bins, low at the bottom) over the 20-frame episode. Touch: an 8×8 patch of skin on the hand, lit when the hand touches the toy. Proprioception: the sensed hand position (filled dot) versus the true position (ring). Vision parameters follow published infant acuity and contrast sensitivity; hearing, touch and proprioception parameters are coarse tables that respect the known order of maturation (see Scope and limitations). The page accepts ?age=6 to open at a given age.

About this tool

What it showsThe input to each sense model at age a, for the same scene. Parameters (acuity, blur sigma, contrast gain, colour saturation, frequency blur, interaural difference, receptive-field blur, noise) are printed under each panel.
Data sourceOne stored adult-quality episode in data/episodes.json (exported by scripts/export_site_data.py). Filter parameter tables are stored in the same file.
CodeFilters re-implemented in JavaScript in docs/assets/app.js (functions filterVision, filterAudio, filterTouch); the reference implementation is dwm/senses.py and tables are in dwm/capability.py.
BackgroundMethods: front ends and Theory: biological mapping.

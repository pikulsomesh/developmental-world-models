The project has two papers. One is about how the models learn; the other is about what babies sense at each age and how age-tuned models and the Baby Senses Lab simulate it.

Paper 1 · Learning Like Infants: Input Constraints in Predictive World Models

The machine-learning paper: how predictive world models learn when their input is degraded the way a baby's senses are, and what that does to learning speed, growth, fusion and expectations about hidden objects.

Read the PDF · Wiki

Paper 2 · Simulating Infant Senses with Age-Tuned Networks

A developmental-neuroscience paper on sensory perception (not cognition): touch, taste and smell, hearing and vision from birth to 24 months, how age-tuned neural networks give a quick simulation of it, and the Baby Senses Lab.

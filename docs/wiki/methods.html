Methods

Environment, front-end operators, training protocol, read-outs, statistics and compute accounting, with the equations used in the code. Implementation files are linked in each section.

Environment: a synthetic multisensory nursery

Episodes last T=20 frames. A toy of type c\in\{1,\dots,4\} moves with constant velocity and bounces in a 32×32 scene; the hand's position follows a smoothed random motor command u_t\in\mathbb R^2 and stays in the lower half. Half the episodes contain a vertical screen. Training sets have 3,000 episodes per seed and test sets 400. Source: dwm/env.py.

Front-end operators

All front ends are fixed functions \tilde o=\Phi_s(o;a) whose parameters are linearly interpolated in age a (months) from Table 1. Source: dwm/senses.py, tables in dwm/capability.py. The age slider applies the same operators in the browser.

Vision

With acuity \mathrm{cpd}(a) in cycles per degree, contrast sensitivity \mathrm{CS}(a) and colour saturation \gamma(a):

\sigma(a)=\tfrac12\cdot\frac{30}{\mathrm{cpd}(a)}\cdot\frac{32}{224},\qquad x=G_{\sigma(a)}*o,\qquad x\leftarrow \bar x+\kappa(a)\,(x-\bar x),\qquad \kappa(a)=\mathrm{clip}\!\left(\tfrac12\log_{10}\mathrm{CS}(a),\,0.15,\,1\right)
\tilde o=\mathrm{clip}_{[0,1]}\big(\ell+\gamma(a)(x-\ell)\big),\qquad \ell=0.299x_R+0.587x_G+0.114x_B

G_\sigma is a separable Gaussian with replicate padding and \bar x the image mean. The log compression of contrast replaces a linear gain CS/100, which would leave a newborn 2% of contrast; contrast sensitivity is a threshold, not a gain.

Hearing

Per ear, x=g_{\beta(a)}*o along frequency, then a high-frequency roll-off, then reduced interaural difference, then additive Gaussian noise \eta(a):

x_f\leftarrow x_f\,\mathrm{sigmoid}\big((h(a)\cdot32-f)/1.5\big),\qquad x_{L,R}\leftarrow m+\rho(a)(x_{L,R}-m),\quad m=\tfrac12(x_L+x_R)

Touch and proprioception

Receptive-field blur G_{\tau(a)} on the 8×8 patch plus noise for touch; additive noise for proprioception.

Training protocol

Adam, learning rate 2\times10^{-3}, batch 32, 2,000 updates per stage for vision and 1,500 for the other senses, four stages in one continuous run. Weights carry over between stages and a fresh Adam optimiser is started at each stage, so all regimes see the same optimiser schedule. Hyperparameters were set once on the immature-input regime and shared by all regimes. Seeds: n = – per regime for the sense models; fusion uses the grown regime's checkpoints. Source: scripts/run_experiments.py.

Regimes

The six regimes (immature input, immature input + growth, growth only, adult input, shuffled input quality, reversed immaturity) are defined in Study design. In code they are the identifiers curriculum_only, grown, growth_only, adult_scratch, shuffled_order and reversed_immaturity.

Read-outs

Linear probes

For features X (a latent, or a memory window of latents) and targets Y, ridge regression with centred targets and an unpenalised intercept,

\hat B=\arg\min_B\lVert (Y-\bar Y)-X_cB\rVert^2+\lambda\lVert B\rVert^2=(X_c^\top X_c+\lambda I)^{-1}X_c^\top(Y-\bar Y)

with X_c standardised features, is fitted on training episodes and evaluated on test episodes with R^2=1-\sum\lVert y-\hat y\rVert^2/\sum\lVert y-\bar y\rVert^2. Identity uses one-hot targets and the arg-max (chance 0.25). Touch is probed on contact frames and proprioception on hand position. Adult-input probes use adult-quality input; own-age probes use the input quality of the checkpoint's age; newborn-input probes are fitted on adult-quality input and tested on newborn-quality input. Source: ridge and probe in dwm/train.py.

Information ceiling

For each sense and age a\in\{0,1,2,3,4,6,9,12\}, a supervised network (the same encoder family) is trained for 1,500 updates to predict toy position and identity from a single filtered frame, and evaluated on held-out frames. This estimates what the filtered input contains.

Violation of expectation

Surprise is one-step prediction error, S_t=\lVert d(\hat z_t)-\tilde o_t\rVert^2 (unimodal) or \lVert r_s(\hat h_t)-z^s_t\rVert^2 per channel (fused, standardised latents), averaged over the hidden stretch and the two frames after expected re-emergence. With S_{\text{pos}} and S_{\text{imp}} the mean surprise on paired possible and impossible events,

\mathrm{VoE}=\frac{S_{\text{imp}}-S_{\text{pos}}}{S_{\text{imp}}+S_{\text{pos}}},\qquad d_z=\frac{\overline{S_{\text{imp}}-S_{\text{pos}}}}{\mathrm{sd}(S_{\text{imp}}-S_{\text{pos}})}

A positive index means the model is more surprised when the toy fails to reappear.

Occluded tracking and fill-in

The hidden toy's horizontal position is probed on fully hidden frames of the track events from the vision model's memory window (k=6 latents), from the hearing latent and from the fused state, fitting on half of the episodes and testing on the other half. Fill-in probes toy position from the fused state with all senses, without vision, and from single senses. Source: scripts/analyze_fusion_voe.py.

Statistics

Bands and whiskers are ±1 s.d. over seeds. Planned comparisons at the 12-month checkpoint use Welch's t-test and Hedges' g with an approximate 95% interval:

g=\frac{\bar a-\bar b}{s_p}\Big(1-\frac{3}{4(n_a+n_b)-9}\Big),\qquad g\pm1.96\sqrt{\frac{n_a+n_b}{n_an_b}+\frac{g^2}{2(n_a+n_b)}}

Per-cell 95% bootstrap intervals are in results/extra.json. No correction for multiple comparisons is applied; all planned comparisons are listed in Results. With few seeds per regime, several comparisons have large effect sizes that are not significant; they are reported as estimates (see Scope and limitations). Source: scripts/analyze_extra.py.

Compute accounting

Training compute is approximated by the number of parameter-updates, \sum_{\text{stages}}(\text{parameters})\times(\text{updates}). Growing models are smaller early in training, so relative to a fixed 12-month-size network they use a fraction of this quantity; the measured ratios are in Results.

Milestones

For each sense, the half-ceiling age is the first age at which the information ceiling reaches half of its 12-month value (position R² for vision and hearing; identity accuracy above chance for touch). Kendall's \tau compares the resulting order with the infant order touch < hearing < vision (Gottlieb, 1971). At five seeds this measure does not recover the infant order (τ = 0.00), because the vision ceiling is already high at birth. The later arrival of vision in the learned curves is set by the front-end tables and is not a finding about learning.
